# Analytical methods

Start with the business decision, observation grain, eligibility and source quality. Preserve operationally meaningful missingness and extremes. Compare distributions, rates and absolute contributions with sample sizes.

For carrier standardization, distinguish the original-population contrast from the unadjusted retained-subset contrast and the standardized contrast within that subset. Tightening strata changes coverage; it is not automatically an improvement in generalizability.

For time comparisons, paired day-block resampling preserves shared daily shocks and short-run dependence approximately. Report the seed, repetitions, block lengths, monthly stratification and what the interval represents. A complete historical file does not require a sampling interval to establish its observed counts.

Separate recorded categories, statistical associations, hypotheses and proposed interventions. Do not infer costs, aircraft defects or independent causal mechanisms from unavailable data.
