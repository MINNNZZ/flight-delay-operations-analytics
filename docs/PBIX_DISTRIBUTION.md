# Power BI distribution status

The two English/Vietnamese PBIX reports were generated, opened in Desktop and checked against 21 baseline KPIs per language. Seven screenshots per edition and the model/report source definitions are available in this repository.

**The PBIX binaries have not been uploaded to GitHub.** They embed flight records, not merely charts or aggregate screenshots. Both are about 223 MiB. Original data redistribution rights have not been independently established, and explicit approval to publish those embedded records is pending.

This source-only release provides code, written reports, DAX, Power Query, PBIP definitions, result tables and screenshots. References to a PBIX in historical reports describe the locally validated deliverable, not a binary download available in this release.

Do not assume that omitting raw CSVs removes the data from a PBIX. Before distributing an embedded report, confirm the rights for the contained dataset and approve that dataset-bearing payload specifically. Until then, use the screenshot galleries and source definitions to review the portfolio.
